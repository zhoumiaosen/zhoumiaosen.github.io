PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

Miaosen Zhou

Advancing robot intelligence through Vision-Language-Action models, machine learning, and real-world autonomy.

I am an AI/ML and Robotics Researcher pursuing a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at San Jose State University, with a background in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Toronto Metropolitan University. My expertise spans artificial intelligence, robotics, machine learning, and intelligent autonomous systems.

My work focuses on robotics, machine learning, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, reinforcement learning, autonomous navigation, and computer vision. I have hands-on experience designing and building single-arm and dual-arm robotic systems, collecting and training robotic datasets, fine-tuning foundation models, and deploying AI solutions on real-world robotic platforms. My research interests include large language models (LLMs), vision-language models (VLMs), transformer architectures, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, robot learning, 3D mapping, 3D environment reconstruction, and autonomous systems. I am passionate about developing intelligent robots that can perceive, reason, and interact effectively in complex real-world environments.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Artificial intelligence, robotics, machine learning, and intelligent autonomous systems.

My research focuses on developing intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, learn, and interact effectively in complex real-world environments.

01

Robotics

Research and development of intelligent robotic systems that integrate perception, learning, and control. My interests include Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, robot learning, robotic manipulation, human-robot interaction, and real-world robotic deployment.

02

Machine Learning & Foundation Models

Development of machine learning methods for prediction, data denoising, system modeling, and decision-making. My research includes LLMs, VLMs, transformer architectures, RAG, AI agents, reinforcement learning, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques such as LoRA.

03

Intelligent Autonomous Systems

Design of autonomous robotic and vehicle systems capable of operating in dynamic environments. Areas of interest include autonomous navigation, path planning, object detection, lane detection, localization, mapping, and decision-making for real-world applications.

04

Data Engineering & AI Infrastructure

Data-centric approaches for building robust AI systems, including robotic dataset collection, data augmentation, feature extraction, multimodal data processing, synthetic data generation, and scalable AI training pipelines.

05

System Modeling & Optimization

Mathematical and computational modeling of complex engineering systems, including system identification, optimization, simulation, signal processing, control systems, and digital-twin technologies for intelligent systems.

06

Emerging Technologies

Exploration of next-generation computing paradigms, including quantum computing, embodied AI, agentic systems, and AI-assisted software engineering for robotics and autonomous systems.
